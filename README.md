## What it is

Zaaheen is a desktop app that gives your AI assistants one private, lasting memory about you. Tell one of them your preferences, your work or your plans, and the others know it too, without you repeating yourself in every app.

Your memories are stored and encrypted on your own computer and are never uploaded. Your Zaaheen account is only for signing in and your subscription; it never holds your memories.

## Why people use it

- **One memory, every app.** Claude, Cursor, ChatGPT and other apps that support MCP all read from and add to the same memory.
- **Stays on your computer.** Memories are encrypted with a key that never leaves your device. Our servers could not read them if they tried.
- **No online AI, no training.** The small models that find and tidy your memories run on your own computer. Nothing is sent to an online AI, and nothing is used to train one.
- **Yours to take or destroy.** Download a copy of everything at any time, or delete it all in one step.
