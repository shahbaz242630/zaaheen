Open-source licences

What Zaaheen uses

Open source

Zaaheen includes open-source software and AI models made by other people and companies. Each is used under its own licence, such as the MIT and Apache 2.0 licences, and those licences, not our Terms of Service, govern those parts. We are grateful to everyone who makes them.

Where to find the notices

With the app

The full notices, with each licence's text and its authors' copyright lines, ship with the Zaaheen app in the folder where it is installed. You can also email customerservice@zaaheen.com and we will send you a copy.
