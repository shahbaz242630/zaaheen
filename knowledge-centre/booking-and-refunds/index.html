Booking and Refund Policy

Each coaching session reserves 90 minutes of the coach's time for you alone. This page explains how to move a session, what happens if you miss it, and when we refund. You see these terms, in bold, before you pay.

Last updated 27 September 2026

The short version

  • You pay the full price, in UAE dirhams (AED), when you book.
  • No refund if you change your mind, cannot attend or miss your session.
  • To move your session, email us at least 24 hours before it starts. You can move it free to any open time within 90 days. Less than 24 hours before, it cannot be moved.
  • If you have not joined within 15 minutes of the start, the session counts as used.
  • If we cancel, or cannot run the session, you choose a full refund or a new time.
  • If you live in the UK or the EU, the law lets you cancel within 14 days of booking for a full refund, as long as your session has not taken place.
  • This policy covers coaching only. The Zaaheen app has its own policies.

Your booking

Paying

When you book

You pay the full price of the session when you book, in UAE dirhams (AED). Your booking is confirmed when your payment has gone through and we have emailed you the date, the time and the Microsoft Teams link. Each payment is for one session at one time.

No refund for a change of mind

Our rule

We do not refund a session because you change your mind, no longer need it, cannot attend or miss it. When you book, we hold that time for you alone and turn other people away from it, and we cannot fill it at short notice. That is why we do not refund.

This does not change the cases where we always refund, set out below, or your legal rights.

Changes and missed sessions

Moving your session

24 hours or more before

If you email us at knowledgecentre@zaaheen.com at least 24 hours before your session starts, we move it free to any open time you choose within 90 days of the original date. You can do this more than once, as long as each request is at least 24 hours before the session it moves. If we cannot offer you any time within those 90 days, we refund you in full.

Moving a session is the way to change your plans. It is not exchanged for money.

Less than 24 hours before

Under 24 hours

Less than 24 hours before the start, a session cannot be moved or cancelled. It goes ahead at the booked time, and if you do not attend it counts as used.

Sending someone else

24 hours or more before

If you cannot attend, you can give your session to someone else. Email us their name and email address at least 24 hours before the start. The same terms then apply to them.

Late or missed sessions

15 minutes

Sessions start and end at the booked time. If you join late, the session still ends at the booked time. If you have not joined within 15 minutes of the start, the session counts as used and is not refunded. The coach waits in the meeting and messages you during those 15 minutes, and we keep a record of this.

Please test Microsoft Teams on your device before the session. A problem with your own device or internet connection counts as a missed session.

When we always refund

If we cancel

Your choice

If we cancel or move your session, or the coach does not attend or cannot run it, including because the coach's own equipment or connection fails, you choose either a full refund or a new time. If only part of the session was lost, you choose extra time or a refund of that part.

We refund to the card you paid with within 14 days of your choice. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.

Events outside our control

Your choice

If the session cannot take place because of something neither of us could control, such as a widespread failure of Microsoft Teams or of the internet, you choose a full refund or a new time. This does not cover your own device or connection, which counts as a missed session.

If a session is not done properly

Within 14 days

We run every session with reasonable care and skill, and as we describe it. If we did not, tell us within 14 days of the session and say what went wrong. We will first offer to run it again, free. If we cannot, or the repeat does not put it right, we refund part or all of the price, depending on how much of the session you received.

Your legal rights

UK and EU: 14 days to cancel

UK and EU consumers

If you live in the United Kingdom or the European Union and book for yourself, not for your business, the law gives you 14 days from the day you book to cancel, without giving a reason.

  • If you cancel before your session has taken place, we refund the full price.
  • If your session falls within those 14 days, we ask you before you pay to confirm that you want it to go ahead. If you then cancel during the session, you pay for the part already delivered. Once the session has been delivered in full, you can no longer cancel.

To cancel, use the Withdraw from contract here link in your booking confirmation email, or email knowledgecentre@zaaheen.com before the 14 days end. You can use the form below, but you do not have to. We confirm by email straight away and refund within 14 days, to the card you paid with, at no cost to you.

Your other rights

Everyone

Nothing in this policy takes away any right the law gives you where you live. The full terms of your booking are in the Coaching Terms.

If you have a problem, please email us before you ask your bank to reverse a payment. We reply within 2 working days, and it is quicker for both of us.
