Obsidian or a memory app for your AI?

Obsidian is a notes app, and a very good one: your notes are files on your computer that you write and read. A memory app is written to by your AI apps as you talk to them, and searched by them later. Use Obsidian when you want to write the knowledge yourself; use a memory app when you want your AI apps to remember you without you keeping notes.

By Zaaheen · Last updated 13 October 2026

The short answer

People use Obsidian as AI memory in two ways: pasting notes into a chat, or connecting the vault to an AI app through a community plugin or a tool like Basic Memory. It works, and many people like it, because every memory is a note they can open and fix. The trade is that you look after the notes, and connecting AI apps to them takes some setup.

Obsidian as AI memory

Obsidian is free for personal use, "Free without limits" in its own words. Sync between devices costs $4 a month billed yearly, and Obsidian says synced notes are protected with AES-256 end-to-end encryption, "preventing us from reading it" (Obsidian's pricing). It runs on phones too.

Obsidian itself doesn't connect to AI apps. For Claude or Cursor to read and write your vault you add a community plugin, or a separate tool such as Basic Memory, which is free, open source and writes plain Markdown that Obsidian can open (Basic Memory's site). That tool needs Python and some setup.

A memory app

A memory app is built for the AI to use. Your apps save short facts as you talk, like a preference, a deadline or a person's name, and look them up later without being asked to open a note. Zaaheen, which we make, keeps that memory encrypted on your computer and shares it between Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Cursor, the ChatGPT app, Codex and other apps. You can see and delete your memories in the app, but you don't write them as notes.

Using both

They don't compete as much as it looks. Plenty of people keep their notes and documents in Obsidian and let a memory app handle the small, personal facts their AI apps keep asking for.
