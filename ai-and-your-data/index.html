AI and Your Data

Zaaheen uses AI to keep your memories easy to find. All of it runs on your own computer. Here is exactly what it does, and what it never does.

The short version

  • Zaaheen's AI runs on your computer. Your memories are never sent to an online AI service by Zaaheen.
  • It finds the right memories for your AI apps. It doesn't chat with you or write answers.
  • Tidying up is off until you turn it on, and it never deletes a memory.
  • Your memories are never used to train AI, by us or for anyone else.

The AI inside Zaaheen

What the AI in Zaaheen does

Three small models

Zaaheen uses three small AI models, each with one job:

  • Understanding meaning, so a search for "my coffee order" also finds "I drink flat whites". It comes with the app.
  • Ranking results, so the most relevant memories come first. The app downloads it the first time it starts.
  • Tidying up, which merges duplicates and settles contradictions. The app downloads it only when you turn tidying on, about 2.5 GB, once.

Where it runs

On your computer

All three run on your own computer, using its processor. Once downloaded, they work offline, and your memories never pass through an online AI service on the way. That is why Zaaheen can promise that we never see your memories: there is no server in the middle to see them.

Zaaheen finds facts, your AI app answers

Your AI apps

When an AI app such as Claude or ChatGPT asks Zaaheen a question, Zaaheen hands back the memories that match, as they are stored. It doesn't make up an answer or talk to you. The AI app you are using writes the reply, using those facts.

Tidying up

What tidying changes

Consolidation

Tidying up merges near-duplicates into one memory, settles facts that contradict each other in favour of the newer one, and moves memories you never use out of everyday recall. It also writes short summaries of each topic and adds other ways of phrasing a memory, so your AI apps find it whatever words they use.

It works only from what you have saved and never deletes a memory. Like any AI, it can occasionally get something wrong, so check anything important before relying on it.

You decide when it runs

Settings

Tidying is off unless you switch on Keep my vault tidy automatically. You choose every day or once a week and the time, or run it yourself whenever you like. You can turn it off again at any time.

Our promises

Your memories never train AI

Always

We never use your memories to train AI models, and we never give them to anyone who would. We couldn't even if we wanted to: they never reach us.

What your AI apps send to their makers

Your choice

The AI apps you connect are made by other companies. When one uses a memory to answer you, it may send that memory to its maker's servers as part of your conversation. What happens next, including whether your conversations train their models, depends on that company's policy and your settings with them. You choose which apps to connect, and you can remove Zaaheen from any of them at any time.

More about what we handle is in our Privacy Policy.
