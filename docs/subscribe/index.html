How to subscribe to Zaaheen

Create your account, download Zaaheen and sign in from the app: your 30-day free trial starts, with no card. Subscribe whenever you are ready, for $5 a month or $48 a year, in the app or on your Account page. One subscription covers your account on every computer you sign in on.

    1. Create your account

    Your browser

    Create your Zaaheen account with your email or Google. We send a code to your email to confirm it's you. Already have an account? Sign in.

    2. Download Zaaheen

    Windows or Mac

    Download the app for your computer and install it. The Getting started guide walks through the install.

    Download for Windows Download for Mac

    3. Sign in from the app

    Zaaheen

    Open Zaaheen and choose Begin set up, then sign in with the same email or Google account. Your 30-day free trial starts the first time you sign in from the app. No card is needed, and everything works during the trial.

    4. Subscribe in the app

    Settings › Account

    Subscribe any time, during your trial or after it:

    1. In Zaaheen, open Settings, then Account.
    2. Under Subscribe, choose $5 a month or $48 a year (save $12).
    3. Payment opens in your browser on our secure checkout. Tax is added at checkout where it applies.
    4. Finish paying in your browser, then go back to Zaaheen. It checks for your payment by itself and shows your plan as subscribed as soon as the payment comes through, usually within a minute. You can also choose I've paid to check straight away.

    In the last seven days of your trial, the home screen also shows a Subscribe button. If your trial ends first, nothing is deleted: Zaaheen asks you to subscribe, and your memories are still there on your computer.

    Or subscribe on the website

    Account page

    Prefer to pay first? Open your Account page, sign in, and under Subscribe choose $5 a month or $48 a year (save $12). Payment opens on the same secure checkout. Your subscription belongs to your account, so the app picks it up when you sign in with the same email.

    Change or cancel your plan

    App or Account page

    Use Manage subscription to change your plan or update your card, and Cancel subscription to stop it. Both are in the app under Settings, Account, and on your Account page. After you cancel, Zaaheen keeps working until the end of the time you've paid for. See the Refund Policy for refunds.
