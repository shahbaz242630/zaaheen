    Email us

    Any question

    Write to us at customerservice@zaaheen.com. If the link doesn't open your email, copy the address:

    customerservice@zaaheen.com

    What to include

    So we can help the first time
    • Windows or Mac.
    • Which AI app you were using.
    • The exact words of any error message.
    • For a problem in the app, its activity record: choose Save activity record… in Settings and attach the file. It shows us the app version and what happened, and contains none of your memories.

    Never send your password or any sign-in code. We will never ask for them.
