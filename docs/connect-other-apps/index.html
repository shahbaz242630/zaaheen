How to connect other AI apps to Zaaheen

Zaaheen connects to AI apps over MCP, the standard way AI apps connect to tools. If your app has its own guide below, use that. Otherwise give your app the setting from Zaaheen's Agents tab, which shows it for your computer exactly.

    What your app needs

    MCP

    Your AI app needs to support MCP servers that run on your computer. Using Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Cursor, ChatGPT, Codex or Antigravity? Use its own guide instead. For any other app, follow the steps below.

    Please follow both steps. If you skip one, your app may not use Zaaheen.

    Step 1: Add it yourself

    Your app

    The easiest way to get the setting right: in Zaaheen, open Agents, choose + Connect an AI app, then Another app. It shows the setting with this computer's own path, with a Copy button. Use the Copy buttons rather than selecting text by hand.

    The path below is an example. It will look like C:\Program Files\Zaaheen\zaaheen.exe, which is where Zaaheen usually installs on Windows. If you installed Zaaheen somewhere else, such as another drive or folder, your path is different, and this one will not work. Copy your exact path from Zaaheen: Agents, + Connect an AI app, Another app, then Copy.

    Give your app this setting:

    {
  "mcpServers": {
    "zaaheen": {
      "command": "C:\\Program Files\\Zaaheen\\zaaheen.exe",
      "args": ["mcp", "serve"]
    }
  }
}

    The path below is an example. It will look like /Applications/Zaaheen.app/Contents/MacOS/zaaheen, which is where Zaaheen usually installs on a Mac. If you installed Zaaheen somewhere else, your path is different, and this one will not work. Copy your exact path from Zaaheen: Agents, + Connect an AI app, Another app, then Copy.

    Give your app this setting:

    {
  "mcpServers": {
    "zaaheen": {
      "command": "/Applications/Zaaheen.app/Contents/MacOS/zaaheen",
      "args": ["mcp", "serve"]
    }
  }
}

    Step 2: Check it's connected

    Your app

    Your app's list of tools or MCP servers shows Zaaheen.
