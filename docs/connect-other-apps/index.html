How to connect other AI apps to Zaaheen

Zaaheen connects to AI apps over MCP, the standard way AI apps connect to tools. Give your app the setting below, or copy it from the Agents tab in Zaaheen, which shows it for your computer exactly.

What your app needs

MCP

Your AI app needs to support MCP servers that run on your computer. We have tested Zaaheen with Claude, Cursor and ChatGPT, and each has its own guide. Other apps that support MCP should work the same way. The Agents tab in Zaaheen has step-by-step instructions for more apps.

The setting

Agents › Another app

Most apps take the setting in this form. Add it where your app keeps its MCP servers, save it, then restart the app:

{
  "mcpServers": {
    "zaaheen": {
      "command": "C:\\Program Files\\Zaaheen\\zaaheen.exe",
      "args": ["mcp", "serve"]
    }
  }
}

That is where Zaaheen usually installs. In Zaaheen, the Agents tab, then + Connect an AI app, then Another app shows the setting for your computer exactly, ready to copy.

Check it worked

Your app

Your app's list of tools or MCP servers shows Zaaheen. While the app is open and connected, it also shows in Zaaheen's Agents tab.
