Security

Zaaheen is built so that your memories are safe even if something goes wrong at our end, because they are never there. Here is how it protects them.

The short version

  • Your memories are encrypted on your computer, and the key never leaves it.
  • Only apps on your own computer can connect to Zaaheen, never anything from the internet.
  • Our server holds no memories, so a break-in there could not expose them.
  • Delete everything destroys the key, so the encrypted files can never be unlocked again, even from a backup of your computer.

On your computer

Your memories are encrypted

At rest

Your memories are encrypted on disk, and so is everything Zaaheen builds from them: the index it uses to search them and the summaries it writes when tidying up. Someone who copied the folder would see only scrambled data.

The key never leaves your computer

Windows

The key that unlocks your memories is created on your computer and kept in Windows' own secure store for your user account. It is never uploaded, never sent to us, and never written into the memory folder itself.

Only apps on your computer can connect

Local only

Your AI apps talk to Zaaheen through a private channel inside your computer. Zaaheen refuses connections from other computers and opens nothing to the internet, and each app has to introduce itself before Zaaheen answers.

This also means Zaaheen cannot protect your memories from harmful software already running on your computer, or from someone who can sign in to your Windows account. Keep Windows up to date and your account protected with a password or PIN.

Delete everything means everything

Settings › Your memories

Delete everything destroys the key first, then the memory files. Without the key, any encrypted copy of those files left on the disk or in a backup of your computer can never be unlocked again. Files you saved with Download my memories are readable copies that are not affected, so delete them yourself.

Online

Signing in

Your browser

You sign in through your own web browser, so the app never sees your password. It receives a sign-in token, kept in Windows' secure store, which it uses only to check your subscription. Signing in has nothing to do with the key to your memories.

Our server holds no memories

Our account service

Our account service knows who you are, when your trial started, whether you have paid and the choices you made at sign-up. It never receives your memories, so there is nothing there to leak. Everything between the app, our websites and our service travels encrypted.

Reporting a problem

What we are still working on

Beta

While Zaaheen is in beta, its Windows installer is not yet code-signed, which is why Windows says the publisher is unknown when you install it. Signing comes before the full launch.

Found a security issue?

Email

If you think you have found a security problem in Zaaheen or our websites, email customerservice@zaaheen.com with "Security" in the subject and tell us how to reproduce it. We reply within 5 working days and keep you updated until it is fixed.

Please give us 90 days to fix it before telling anyone else, do not access or change other people's data, and do not disrupt our services. If you follow these rules, we will not take legal action against you for your research, and we will thank you publicly if you would like.
