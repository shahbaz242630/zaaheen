Privacy Policy

How Zaaheen handles personal information when you enquire about our AI Readiness Assessment and when we work with your business. It covers what we collect, why, who helps us, how long we keep it and your rights.

Last updated 26 November 2026

The short version

  • We collect only what we need to answer your enquiry and, if you hire us, to run the assessment.
  • We contact you only because you ticked "Please contact me". Updates about AI for business are sent only if you tick the second box.
  • Enquiries are deleted after 12 months, whether or not we work together.
  • In interviews we record job roles and processes, not people's names or personal details, and we never contact your customers.
  • Interview notes are deleted 90 days after we deliver the report, unless you ask us in writing to keep them for follow-on work.
  • We never sell your information or use it for advertising, and we set no cookies on the form.
  • To see, correct or delete your information, email consulting@zaaheen.com. We reply within 30 days.

About this policy

What it covers

The AI Readiness Assessment

This policy covers the AI Readiness Assessment only: the enquiry form at consulting.zaaheen.com, the emails we exchange with you, and the assessments we carry out for businesses.

The Zaaheen app has its own Privacy Policy, and coaching in the Knowledge Centre has its own Coaching Privacy Notice.

Who is responsible

Controller

Zaaheen Artificial Intelligence Developing Services, trading as Zaaheen, is responsible for your enquiry, for our relationship with you as a client, and for our invoices. In data protection law, that makes us the controller.

Information about your staff that we gather in interviews is different. Your company decides why the assessment happens, so your company is the controller of that information, and we act on its behalf under the data processing agreement in our engagement letter.

What we collect

When you enquire

Enquiry form

What you type into the form: your name, work email, phone number (the country code and number, kept as one international number), company, country, number of staff, estimated annual turnover and sector. If you choose to, you can also give a best time to call and add comments.

We also keep your two answers about contact, and when you sent the form. The "Please contact me" tick is required. The tick for occasional updates is optional and never ticked for you.

Your enquiry is stored in our enquiry database and a copy is emailed to our own mailbox, so we can read it and reply. You need to fill in the required fields for us to answer you; the best time to call, the comments and the updates tick are up to you.

We do not store your network address or details about your browser with your enquiry. Our system logs record error codes, never what you typed.

The bot check and daily limit

Before your enquiry is accepted

Before the form is sent, a bot check run by Cloudflare confirms you are a person. To decide, it looks at technical signals from your browser and at your network address. We pass your network address to Cloudflare for the check and do not keep it; we receive only the answer: person or not. Cloudflare explains how it handles that information in its own privacy notice.

To stop the form being flooded, we count how many enquiries come from each visitor in a day. The count is filed under a scrambled code made from your network address, the date and a secret key. The code cannot be turned back into your address, it changes every day so it cannot link your visits, and the count is deleted the next day.

If you become a client

If we work together

The names, job titles, work emails and phone numbers of the people we deal with at your company, our engagement letter and other engagement documents, and invoices and payment records.

We use these to agree the work, arrange visits, send the report and invoice your company. We need them to carry out our contract with your company.

In interviews

On site

We sit with your teams to see how the work is done. We record job roles and processes: the steps, how long they take, volumes, hand-offs and what gets in the way. We record roles, not names, and delete any name we come across. We do not record personal details.

We use what your staff tell us to understand the work, not to judge the people doing it. The report describes teams and roles, not individuals.

If you share sample documents, we ask you to hide customer names and contact details first. We never contact your customers.

How we use it

Why, on what basis, for how long

Our reasons

We follow the UAE's personal data protection law (Federal Decree-Law 45 of 2021). Each use of your information rests on one of the reasons that law allows:

WhatWhy, and on what basisHow long
Your enquiry To reply and call you about it. Your consent: the required "Please contact me" tick. 12 months from arrival, then deleted
The enquiry email in our mailbox The same reason, on the same consent. 12 months
Your choice about updates To send occasional updates about AI for business. Your consent: the optional tick. Until you withdraw it or the enquiry is deleted, whichever comes first
The daily visitor count To keep the form working for real enquiries, as part of handling the enquiry you consented to. It holds a scrambled code, not your address. Deleted the next day
Client contacts and engagement documents To agree and run the assessment. Our contract with your company. 7 years

Invoices and payment records To keep accounts. The law requires it. 7 years

You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing us. We then stop contacting you. Withdrawing does not undo what we did with your information before you asked.

Occasional updates

Only if you ask

We send occasional updates about AI for business only if you tick the box for it on the form. You can stop them at any time by replying to one or by emailing us. Your choice ends when your enquiry is deleted after 12 months, so updates stop then unless you ask again.

What we never do

Never

We never sell your information or share it for advertising. We never contact your customers. We make no decisions about you by automated means.

Who helps us

Our service providers

Under contract

These companies process information for us, only on our instructions:

  • Cloudflare: runs our website, the enquiry form, the bot check and the database that holds enquiries.
  • Microsoft: our email. Each enquiry is emailed to our own mailbox, consulting@zaaheen.com.
  • Hostinger: hosts the private, encrypted app we use to record interview notes.

We also use AI tools that help us analyse notes and draft reports, used only with settings that do not use your information to train them.

We may also share information where the law requires it, or to deal with a legal claim.

Data outside your country

Safeguards

We are based in the United Arab Emirates. Our providers may store your information outside your country. We choose providers that are bound by contract to protect it. When we work in Saudi Arabia or Oman, we follow their rules for sending personal information abroad.

How we protect it

Safeguards

Everything travels over encrypted connections. Interview notes are encrypted in our private app. Access is limited to the people at Zaaheen who work on your assessment.

We keep as little as we need: the form asks only for what helps us answer you, and interview notes hold roles, not names. If something went wrong that put your information at risk, we would tell you, and the authorities where the law requires it.

Keeping and your rights

How long we keep it

No longer than needed
  • Enquiries: 12 months from the day they arrive, whether or not we work together. A daily job then deletes them from our database. Deleted enquiries can remain in the database's automatic backups for up to 7 more days.
  • Enquiry emails in our mailbox: 12 months.
  • Your choice about updates: until you withdraw it or your enquiry is deleted, whichever comes first.
  • Interview notes: 90 days after we deliver the report, unless you ask us in writing to keep them for follow-on work. Notes kept that way are deleted 90 days after the follow-on work ends.
  • One copy of the final report, engagement documents and invoices: 7 years, for our legal and accounting records.

You can ask us to delete your enquiry sooner at any time.

Your rights

Everyone

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it or delete it. You can stop updates and withdraw your consent at any time. Where the law allows, you can also object to how we use your information, ask us to limit it, or ask for a copy in a form you can take elsewhere.

To ask, email consulting@zaaheen.com. We reply within 30 days. We may need to confirm who you are first. Some records we must keep by law, such as invoices; we tell you if that applies.

If you were interviewed for your employer's assessment, you can ask your employer or us. We act on your employer's instructions, so we pass your request to them.

Complaints

Us first, then a regulator

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, please tell us first at consulting@zaaheen.com, and we will try to put it right. You can also complain to a data protection authority: in the UAE, the UAE's federal data protection authority; in Saudi Arabia, the Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA); elsewhere, the authority in your country.

Children

Businesses only

The AI Readiness Assessment is for businesses. We do not knowingly collect information about children.

Changes to this policy

Dated

When we change this policy, we update the date at the top of this page. If a change matters to a business we are working with, we email it.

Contact us

Email

Zaaheen Artificial Intelligence Developing Services, trading as Zaaheen, Dubai, United Arab Emirates (Dubai trade licence 1651252). For anything about this policy or your information, email consulting@zaaheen.com.
