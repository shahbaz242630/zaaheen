Terms of Service

These terms apply when a business engages Zaaheen for an AI Readiness Assessment, or for the optional help that follows it. Each engagement letter attaches the dated version you agree to.

The short version

  • It starts with a free 30-minute call. If you go ahead, you get a fixed written quote, plus VAT where it applies, valid 30 days, and we both sign an engagement letter.
  • We recommend; we never build, sell or resell tools, and we take no commissions, referral fees or kickbacks from any provider.
  • You pay a 50% first payment when the letter is signed and 50% after the report, each within 14 days, in UAE dirhams plus VAT where it applies.
  • Moving or cancelling a visit follows our Cancellation and Refund Policy: 14 days or more before the original first visit date in your engagement letter, the first payment is refunded in full.
  • We sign a confidentiality agreement before the first visit, record roles rather than names, and delete our notes 90 days after the report.
  • Once the fee is paid in full, you may use the report inside your business and share it with your advisers and the providers you brief. We keep our methods, templates and know-how. We correct factual errors free within 30 days.
  • The report is advice, not a guarantee. Our liability for how we did the work is capped at the fees paid for that engagement, except where UAE law forbids a limit.
  • UAE law applies. We talk first, then the courts of Dubai. There is no arbitration.

About these terms

Who we are

Dubai

Zaaheen is the trading name of Zaaheen Artificial Intelligence Developing Services, a business licensed in Dubai, United Arab Emirates (Dubai trade licence 1651252). The AI Readiness Assessment is one of our services. "We" and "us" mean us. "You" means the business that engages us.

Who these terms are for

Businesses only

Our consulting services are for businesses and other organisations, not for private individuals. The person who signs the engagement letter confirms that they have the authority to bind the business.

These terms cover the assessment, a monthly retainer if you take one, and help choosing and briefing a provider after the report. They do not cover the Zaaheen app or our coaching, which have their own terms.

How the documents fit together

Engagement letter

Each engagement has three documents: the engagement letter, which sets out the scope, fee and dates; these terms; and the confidentiality agreement. The engagement letter wins only where it expressly changes these terms. Otherwise these terms apply.

Each engagement letter names the date of the version of these terms that applies and attaches a copy, so we both know exactly what was agreed. Your own purchase order terms do not apply unless we sign them.

The free call and the quote

The free call

30 minutes

The first call is free and you are under no obligation. You tell us about your business and what is not working, and we tell you honestly whether an assessment is worth it for you. What you tell us on the call stays confidential, even before any agreement is signed.

The written quote

Valid 30 days

If you want to go ahead, we send a fixed quote in writing, plus VAT where it applies, with no hidden costs. It lists what is included, such as the departments, sites, days on site, the report and the read-out meeting, and what is not included. It also sets out how the fee is split across the stages of the work, which matters if the work stops part-way. The quote is valid for 30 days.

The engagement starts when we both sign the engagement letter based on the quote.

What we do and do not do

The assessment

On site

We visit your sites, sit with the people doing the work, interview them by role and map each process with timed steps. We score each step, give each team one of four verdicts, and write a report with our recommendations. We then present it to your team at a read-out meeting. The engagement letter sets out exactly which teams and sites are included.

What the assessment is not

Please read

The assessment is an operational review of how work gets done. It is not a financial audit, an assurance report, legal advice or a certification of any AI system. We do not check your accounts, and we do not certify that any system is safe, lawful or compliant.

Our independence

A binding promise

This is a promise we make in every engagement, not a marketing line. We do not:

  • Sell or resell any AI product, software or hardware.
  • Run an affiliate programme, or take referral fees, commissions or kickbacks from any provider.
  • Act as a partner or reseller of any provider.

We do not build the systems we recommend. Our only income from an engagement is the fee you pay us. If we name a product, it is because it fits your work, and you buy it directly from its maker. If this promise is ever broken, that is a breach of these terms.

Our standard of work

Every engagement

We carry out the work with reasonable care and skill, in good faith, and as the engagement letter describes. We tell you promptly if anything stops us doing what we agreed.

Help after the report

Optional

After the report you may ask us to be your point of contact for AI on a monthly retainer, or to help you choose and brief a provider to put our recommendations in place. Each is a separate, optional piece of work with its own engagement letter, under these terms. It never depends on which tools you choose, and we do not set systems up ourselves.

What we need from you

Access, people and a contact

Agreed times

You give us access to your sites, people and information at the times we agree in the visit plan. You name one contact person who can answer questions and make decisions during the work.

Accurate information

Good faith

You give us accurate information in good faith, and you confirm you are allowed to share it with us. We rely on what you and your staff tell us and show us. We do not audit it.

Telling your staff

Before the visit

You tell your staff about the visit and the interviews before we arrive. We give you a short notice to share, and we explain it again to each person we interview. Please keep customer details out of what you show us where you can.

Site safety

On site

You tell us about your site safety rules and any hazards before we arrive, and we follow them while we are there.

If access or people are missing

Rebooked once

If the access or people we agreed are not available, we rebook that part of the work once per engagement, at no charge. If it happens again, the time we set aside counts as work done. If missing access changes the dates or the work needed, we agree any change with you in writing first.

Fees and payment

Fees and currency

AED

Our fees are fixed in the quote and engagement letter, in UAE dirhams (AED), plus VAT where it applies. Travel and accommodation outside Dubai are always a fixed line in the quote, so there are no hidden costs.

When you pay

Two payments

You pay in two parts:

  • a first payment of 50% of the fee when the engagement letter is signed, which, once received, reserves the dates for you; and
  • the remaining 50% after we deliver the report.

Each invoice is payable within 14 days. The right to use the report passes to you when the fee is paid in full.

VAT and withholding tax

Where it applies

Our prices do not include VAT. We add VAT where the law requires it. Each side handles its own other taxes.

If the law where you are requires you to withhold tax from our fee, for example Saudi withholding tax, you add that amount to the payment so that we receive the full quoted fee, and you send us the receipt for the tax you paid.

Late and disputed invoices

No interest

We do not charge interest on late payments. If an invoice is overdue, we may pause the work after telling you in writing, until it is paid. Dates that slip because of a pause move by the same amount.

If you disagree with an invoice, tell us within 14 days of receiving it and pay the part you do not dispute while we sort out the rest.

Changes, moving and ending

Changing what we do

In writing

If you want to add or remove teams, sites or days, we agree the change and any change to the fee in writing before the work starts. Email is enough.

Moving or cancelling a visit

Cancellation and Refund Policy

Our Cancellation and Refund Policy is part of these terms. It sets out how to move a visit and what happens if you cancel. In short:

  • 14 days or more before the original first visit date in your engagement letter: we refund the first payment in full.
  • 7 to 13 days before: we keep 25% of the total fee.
  • Less than 7 days before: we keep the first payment.
  • After the visits have started: you pay the greater of the work done, using the split written in your quote, and the first payment (50% of the fee). We hand over what we have gathered.

The days are calendar days, counted from the day we receive your email (Dubai time) to the original first visit date. Moving the date does not restart the count. If the first payment has not yet been paid when you cancel, the amount we keep is payable within 14 days.

These amounts reflect the weeks we hold for you and turn other work away from.

Ending an engagement

Either side

Either of us may end an engagement by telling the other in writing. If you end it before the first visit, the cancellation amounts in our Cancellation and Refund Policy apply. If you end it after the visits have started, you pay the greater of the work done up to that point, measured by the split in your quote, and the first payment (50% of the fee). Either way, we hand over everything finished or gathered so far. If we end it without a good reason, we refund everything you paid for work not yet done.

Either of us may also end it straight away if the other seriously breaks these terms and does not put it right within 14 days of being told, or becomes insolvent. If you end it this way because of our breach, the cancellation amounts and the first payment minimum do not apply: you pay only for the work done, and we refund the rest.

Confidentiality and data

The confidentiality agreement

Before the first visit

We both sign a mutual confidentiality agreement before the first visit. It is part of every engagement and continues after the engagement ends. Each side follows the data protection law that applies to it. Our Consulting Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information.

For information about your staff that we gather in interviews, your business is the controller and we act on its behalf under the data processing agreement in the engagement letter.

Our notes and records

90 days

We record roles, not names, and delete any name we come across. We delete our working notes 90 days after we deliver the report, unless you ask us in writing to keep them for follow-on work; notes kept that way are deleted 90 days after the follow-on work ends. We keep one copy of the final report, the engagement documents and our invoices for 7 years, for our legal and accounting records, under the same confidentiality.

Naming you as a client

Your choice

We never name you as a client, or quote your results, without your written agreement.

Our use of AI tools

Reviewed by a person

We may use AI tools to help analyse our notes and draft the report. We do this under our confidentiality duties, and only with settings that do not use your information to train the tools. A person on our team reviews everything before it reaches you.

The report

Using the report

Once paid

Once the fee is paid in full, you may use the report inside your business and share it with your advisers and the providers you brief.

We keep our methods, question sets, scoring, templates and know-how, which we use for all our clients. Using them in your report does not transfer them to you.

What the report is

Our opinion

The report is our professional opinion on the date of the visits, based on what we were shown and told. Costs, savings and payback periods are estimates. AI products and prices change quickly, so check the details with the maker before you buy. The report is written for you; no one else may rely on it unless we agree in writing.

No guaranteed results

Please read

We do not guarantee any result, such as savings, growth or a successful project. Our recommendations are advice. You decide what to do, and you remain responsible for your choices and for how any system is used in your business.

Corrections and the follow-up meeting

30 days

If the report contains a factual error and you tell us within 30 days of delivery, we correct it free. You may also have one free follow-up meeting within 30 days of delivery to go through any questions.

If we did not deliver what your quote and engagement letter describe, tell us within 30 days of delivery and we will put it right, or refund the part not delivered. Here, "delivery" means the day we send you the report.

Disagreeing with our findings or verdicts is not a defect in the report, and it is not a reason to withhold payment.

Responsibility

The limit on our liability

Fees paid

For any claim about how we performed an engagement, such as a late or poor report, our total liability is limited to the fees you paid us for that engagement.

Losses we do not cover

Indirect loss

We are not liable for indirect or consequential loss, such as lost profit, revenue, savings or goodwill, or for loss we could not have foreseen when the engagement letter was signed. We are not liable for the results of decisions you make based on the report.

The cap above and these exclusions of indirect loss apply to your liability to us as well, except for fees you owe us.

What is never limited

UAE law

Nothing in these terms limits or excludes liability that UAE law does not allow to be limited, such as for harmful acts, fraud or gross negligence. That includes liability for deliberate wrongdoing and for death or personal injury.

General

Hiring each other's people

6 months

During an engagement and for 6 months after it ends, neither of us will approach the other's people who worked on the engagement to offer them a job. There is no fee if this happens; it is simply a promise. General job adverts, and people who reply to them, are not covered.

Events outside our control

Either side

Neither of us is responsible for a delay or failure caused by events outside our reasonable control, such as severe weather, a public health emergency, travel bans, government action or a major utility failure. The affected dates move. If either of us cannot perform for more than 90 days because of such an event, either of us may end the engagement: you pay only for the work done, nothing more, and we refund the rest.

Subcontractors

With your agreement

We use a subcontractor on your engagement only if you agree in writing first. Anyone we bring in is bound by the same confidentiality duties as we are, and we remain responsible for their work. This does not cover the service providers and types of tools listed in our Consulting Privacy Policy, which act only on our instructions.

Complaints and disputes

Talk first

If you are unhappy with anything, email consulting@zaaheen.com and tell us what happened. We reply within 5 working days, and a senior member of our team reviews it. Both of us will try in good faith to resolve it within 30 days, including meeting if that helps, before either takes any legal step.

Law and courts

Dubai

These terms and every engagement are governed by the law of the United Arab Emirates as applied in Dubai. If a dispute is not resolved by talking, the courts of Dubai have exclusive jurisdiction. We may instead take it to the DIFC Small Claims Tribunal, but only if both of us agree in writing after the dispute has started. There is no arbitration.

Changes to these terms

New engagements

We may update these terms. A change applies only to engagements signed after it. A signed engagement keeps the version attached to its engagement letter.

Other legal points

Both of us

If any part of these terms is found invalid, the rest still applies. These terms are written in English, and the English version governs; any Arabic copy is a translation unless the engagement letter says otherwise. Notices by email, and engagement letters signed electronically, are valid. If either of us does not enforce a right straight away, we do not give it up.

Contact us

Email

Consulting questions, quotes and complaints: consulting@zaaheen.com. To book the free call, use our enquiry page. Our company details are on our Company Information page.
