# Account, trial and subscription

## The Zaaheen account

- Zaaheen needs an account. The user creates it with their email (a code is emailed to confirm it is them) or with Google, from the app (**Create an account**) or on zaaheen.com.
- Signing in happens in the web browser; the app never sees the password. Signing in never shares the memories: they stay locked on the computer. The account knows who the user is, when the trial started, whether they have paid, and choices made at sign-up. It never receives memories.
- One subscription covers the account on every computer the user signs in on. Memories are kept per computer.

## The free trial

- No card needed. Its length is shown in the app and on zaaheen.com/pricing. It starts the first time the user signs in from the Zaaheen app. Everything works during the trial.
- The top of the app shows "Your N-day free trial has started.", then "N days left in your free trial.", and in the last days "Your free trial ends in N days." with a **Subscribe** button.

## Prices

A monthly plan or a yearly plan (cheaper over a year). The current prices are on the plan buttons in **Settings**, **Account**, and on zaaheen.com/pricing. Tax is added at checkout where it applies. Cancel anytime.

## Subscribing

In the app:
1. Click **Settings**, then **Account**.
2. Under **Subscribe**, click the monthly or the yearly plan button (each shows its price).
3. The payment page opens in the browser. Finish paying there.
4. Go back to Zaaheen. It notices the payment by itself, usually within a minute ("Waiting for your payment"), or click **I've paid** to check straight away. **Not now** stops waiting.

Or on the website: open the Account page (account.zaaheen.com/account/), sign in, and choose a plan under Subscribe. The app picks it up when the user signs in with the same email.

## Changing plan, card, or cancelling

- **Settings**, **Account**, **Manage subscription**: change the plan or update the card (opens in the browser).
- **Settings**, **Account**, **Cancel subscription**: stops it. Zaaheen keeps working until the end of the time already paid for. The user can also cancel on the website Account page, or with the cancel link in any email about the subscription.
- If a payment fails, the app shows "Your last payment didn't go through." with **Update your card**. Everything keeps working meanwhile.

## When the trial or subscription ends

Nothing is deleted. The app shows "Your free trial has ended." (or "Your subscription has ended.") with the plan buttons, and AI apps are told to ask the user to subscribe (topic `messages`). Until the user subscribes, Zaaheen does not serve AI apps. **Download my memories** still works on that screen ("It's yours, whether or not you subscribe."), and so do **Delete my account** and **Sign out**. After paying, Zaaheen unlocks by itself.

Other screens the user may see:
- "Sign in to open your memories.": the user is signed out on this computer. Click **Sign in** (or **Create an account**).
- "We couldn't confirm your subscription.": Zaaheen could not reach the account service (usually no internet). Check the connection and click **Try again**. Only the subscription is checked, never the memories.
- "Zaaheen couldn't open your account on this computer.": click **Close Zaaheen** and open it again. If it keeps happening, email customerservice@zaaheen.com.

## Signing out

**Settings**, **Account**, **Sign out**. The memories stay on the computer; signing in again opens them.

## Refunds

- Ask within the refund window after a payment (stated in the Refund Policy) and that payment is refunded in full, on either plan: email customerservice@zaaheen.com from the address on the Zaaheen account.
- Only the latest payment, one refund per person, no part refunds. After the window, no refund except where the law gives one; cancelling stops future charges.
- Deleting the account cancels the subscription with no refund of the time left: ask for a refund first if still within the window.
- Full policy: Refund Policy, in Settings, Documents.

## Delete my account

**Settings**, **Account**, **Delete my account** (also on the lock screen). It deletes **all** the memories on this computer first (offer **Download my memories** before), after typing **DELETE** to confirm, then opens a page where the user confirms deleting the Zaaheen account. To delete only the account and keep the memories, the user uses the page account.zaaheen.com/delete-account instead. Deleting the account cancels the subscription.
