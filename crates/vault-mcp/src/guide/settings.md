# Settings

Open the Zaaheen app and click **Settings** at the top. The sections are on the left: **Account**, **Your memories**, **Documents**, **Help**, **About Zaaheen**.

## Account

- **Signed in as**: the account's email, and **Sign out**.
- **Your plan**: trial, subscribed, or ended.
- **Subscribe**: the monthly and yearly plan buttons (each shows its price). Payment opens in the browser; tax is added where it applies.
- **Manage subscription**: change the plan or update the card (opens in the browser).
- **Cancel subscription**: stop it; Zaaheen works until the end of the time paid for.
- **Delete my account**: deletes the memories on this computer, then opens a page to confirm deleting the account.
- **Waiting for your payment**: shows while a payment is being finished in the browser, with **I've paid** and **Not now**.

Details: topic `account`.

## Your memories

"Everything you keep is stored on this device and encrypted. It stays on this device even if you uninstall Zaaheen."

- **Memories location**: the folder where they are kept.
- **Move my memories…**: keep them in another folder or on another drive.
  1. Click **Move my memories…** and choose the new folder.
  2. Read the notes shown. If the folder is one a cloud app syncs, Zaaheen asks the user to confirm "No cloud app syncs this folder." (memories should not be in a synced folder).
  3. Click **Move them here**. Zaaheen closes, moves them, and opens again by itself; with many memories this can take a few minutes. "Please keep Zaaheen open until this finishes. If it closes, your memories stay safe where they were."
  4. If the old copy could not be removed yet (for example its drive is unplugged), the page says so; **Stop waiting for the old copy** appears when that drive cannot be seen.
- **Download my memories**: saves everything as one readable file (Markdown), wherever the user chooses. It works whether or not the user subscribes, also from the lock screen.
- **Delete everything**: removes the memories for good; the only action that erases them. Type **DELETE**, then **Permanently delete everything**. It destroys the encryption key, so what remains on disk becomes unreadable to everyone. It cannot be undone, not even from a backup. It does not cancel a subscription (**Manage subscription** is offered for that).

## Documents

Guides and policies on zaaheen.com, each opening in the browser. **Using Zaaheen**: Getting started, Connecting your AI apps, Your memories, Account and subscription, Help. **Policies**: Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Refund Policy, AI and Your Data, Security, Company Information, Open-source licences.

## Help

**If something goes wrong**: Zaaheen keeps a private activity record on the computer (what it did and when, and any errors). It records none of the memories and is never sent anywhere on its own. **Save activity record…** saves a copy as a text file, which the user can email to customerservice@zaaheen.com with a description of the problem.

## About Zaaheen

How the memories are kept: **Encryption** (and where the key is kept), **Vault location**, **Stored** (how many memories, across how many areas), **Recall engine** (on-device, works offline), **Audit log** (whether the history of changes is verified), and **Version**.
